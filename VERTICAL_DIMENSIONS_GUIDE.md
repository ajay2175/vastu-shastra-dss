## Introduction

In traditional Vastu Shastra, the complete directional framework encompasses **10 directions**, not just the 8 horizontal directions commonly referenced:

### The 10-Direction Framework

**Horizontal Plane (8 directions):**
- Cardinal: North (Uttara), East (Purva), South (Dakshina), West (Paschima)
- Intercardinal: Northeast (Ishanya), Southeast (Agneya), Southwest (Nairitya), Northwest (Vayavya)

**Vertical Axis (2 dimensions):**
- **Zenith (Urdhva)** - Upward/Heaven/Spiritual
- **Nadir (Adho)** - Downward/Earth/Foundation

These vertical dimensions represent the complete 3D spatial framework essential for comprehensive Vastu design. They govern the **vertical energy flow** and connect architectural spaces to both cosmic and terrestrial forces.
